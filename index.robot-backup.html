The Payoff

Why automated cleaning pays off

Robotics is not about replacing people — it's about cutting cost per square meter on every contract you run.

One operator, whole fleet

One person supervises every machine at once. Instead of a full daytime cleaning crew, you staff a single operator — and cut labor cost per square meter on every contract.

Consistent, measurable results

A verified 95% pick-up rate on every single run — day shift and night shift included. No bad days, no re-work, no arguments with the client about quality.

Self-servicing fleet

Auto docking handles charging, water fill and drain, plus a 4-minute wastewater tank self-clean. Minimal human intervention between jobs.

Daytime floors stay open

Quiet models run below 60 dB — they clean while your tenants work or sleep. Lobbies and corridors never need to be closed for cleaning again.
